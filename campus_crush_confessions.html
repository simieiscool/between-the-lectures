♡ THE SOFT RULES

Keep it sweet. Keep it safe.

This is a campus crush corner, not a call-out page. The goal is butterflies, not bad days. All submissions stay private to the three admins unless selected and manually posted to Instagram.

01 · No names, no clues that identify someone.

Use general descriptions only. Don't reveal a person's class section, roll number, contact details, schedule or private messages.

02 · No bullying or allegations.

Submissions that shame, threaten, spread rumours or target anyone will be rejected.

03 · Three admins review every note.

Nothing goes public automatically. The admins may edit identifying details or reject a submission. No confession feed is shown on this website.

04 · Your department and year are sensitive context.

They are required for the form but stay private by default. You can separately consent to showing them on an Instagram card. Never include another person's identifying details.

05 · Preview limitation.

This demo stores sample entries only in this browser. It does not send submissions to the three admins or provide real secure storage yet.
