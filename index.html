♡ THE SOFT RULES

Keep it sweet. Keep it safe.

This is a campus crush corner, not a call-out page. Submissions are stored privately and can be read only by the two approved admins. Nothing is published automatically.

01 · No names, no clues that identify someone.

Use general descriptions only. Don't reveal a person's class section, roll number, contact details, schedule or private messages.

02 · No bullying or allegations.

Submissions that shame, threaten, spread rumours or target anyone will be rejected.

03 · Two admins review every note.

Nothing goes public automatically. Admins may edit identifying details or reject a submission. No confession feed is shown on this website.

04 · Your department and year are sensitive context.

They stay private by default. You can separately consent to showing them on an Instagram card. Never include another person's identifying details.

05 · Privacy reminder.

Although the inbox is restricted, please don't submit anything that would put you or someone else at risk if shared. Admins should review carefully and remove identifying clues before posting.
